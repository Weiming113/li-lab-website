Principal Investigator

Weiming Li, Ph.D.

Assistant Professor · Department of Biology · University of Texas at Arlington

Dr. Weiming Li is a bioinformatics scientist with over a decade of experience spanning genome assembly, population genomics, and multi-omics analysis across model organisms, agricultural species, and human disease.

Weiming began pursuing his passion for scientific discovery after he earned his B.S. in Bioengineering from Hubei University of Technology, Wuhan, China (2011). He developed a strong interest in understanding key biological questions utilizing bioinformatics approaches during his master’s program at Huazhong Agricultural University, Wuhan, China (2016). Afterwards, he completed Ph.D. training in bioinformatics and ecology at the Institute of Zoology, Chinese Academy of Sciences (UCAS), Beijing, China (2022), where he studied adaptive evolution in reptiles using reference genomes, population genomics, single-cell transcriptomics, and functional experimental validation. Then, he worked in the labs of Dr. Vishal Nigam and Dr. Ram Savan at Seattle Children’s Hospital / University of Washington (UW), where he investigated the molecular mechanisms of cardiopulmonary bypass (CPB)-induced inflammation and cardiac arrest–related brain injury in neonatal patients, using single-cell and spatial transcriptomic, epigenomic, and chromatin-conformation approaches to identify novel therapeutic targets.
